# Diagnostic-driven amendment: exact donor-label permutation sensitivity

Date: 2026-11-09. Added after inspection of the initial asymptotic results, before calculating any exact permutation P values. This is explicitly a diagnostic-driven sensitivity, not an originally preregistered test.

The prescribed first analysis found a perfect Spearman ordering in the Plasmablasts category with only five eligible donors. SciPy's asymptotic two-sided P was approximately 1.4e-24, which is an unsuitable measure of extreme evidence in such a small sample. The parent authorized a uniform sensitivity for all nine preselected classes, not a selective replacement for this category. All first-run results, executed script, figures and log are preserved in `audit_v16/SINGLE_CELL_CONTEXT_FIRST_ASYMPTOTIC_SNAPSHOT/` with a hash manifest. Original asymptotic columns remain in all final results.

Keep the source, normalized values, donor aggregation, nine author categories, ≥20 cells per aggregate, ≥5 donors per test and fixed nine-comparison BH family unchanged. For each testable category (all have ≤10 eligible donors), enumerate every permutation of the donor labels of the IFI44L ranks relative to fixed IFN-response ranks. Use doubled average ranks minus n+1 as exact integers. Count permutations for which the absolute centred-rank dot product is at least the absolute observed dot product. Two-sided exact P is extreme_count/n!; do not add 1, since enumeration is exhaustive. Repeated rank values caused by ties still participate as distinct donor-label permutations and therefore retain their correct multiplicity. Use blocks of 50,000 permutations to bound memory. No random seed or Monte Carlo approximation is used.

The eight testable lineages receive exhaustive permutation sensitivities. Normal Epithelial, with four eligible donors, remains descriptive/NA under the unchanged eligibility rule. BH uses the same fixed family of nine, internally assigning P=1 to unavailable tests while displaying NA. Preserve total and extreme permutation counts, rank/tie information, original asymptotic P/FDR, exact sensitivity P/FDR and the method distinction. S3 will preferentially display rho, donor n and exact-permutation sensitivity FDR, with a caption identifying the diagnostic-driven nature. Do not imply that seven significant categories establish differences from an eighth non-significant category, that this is independent replication, or that correlations resolve cell origins or mechanisms.
