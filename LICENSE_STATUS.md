A version label does not establish publication or manuscript authorship.

Their original access and redistribution terms continue to apply. The Xena metadata records public access/redistribution permission. Additional file 3 supplies a documented public TCGA subset/header/clinical metadata, Additional file 4 supplies public GSE21653 subset/metadata, Additional file 5 supplies core supplementary methods/figures/robustness and annotation audits; Additional file 6 supplies the v16 methods, Figures S2–S4 and complete extension result/source tables; and Additional files 7–8 jointly supply GSE96058 metadata and two lossless binary transport parts of the exact all-gene subset. Source papers reviewed internally are not repackaged as freely licensed article PDFs. Author names, affiliations, contributions and approval remain separate author decisions and are deferred.
